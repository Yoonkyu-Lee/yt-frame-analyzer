## Observations
- Argument so far: (1) provocation "all code is slop (except mine)" and the comic mocking the "our craft vs their slop" attitude; (2) she ships ~3,000 PRs/month, so volume is not the issue; quality and quantity together is "the new meta"; (3) trust in agents should rise with the number of agents, but most people are stuck in a "human verifies slop" loop, and she wants an "agent writes good code / agent verifies it" loop; (4) the "poteto pyramid": cheap, high-leverage trust measures at the top (codebase quality, static analysis), costliest at the bottom (human review); (5) "code is memory": the codebase teaches agents by example, so workarounds get copied into patterns ("each copy makes the next copy more likely"); "Organic Architecture" is the metaphor for a codebase that grows by accretion; (6) you should still sometimes read the code.
- Claims resting on on-screen numbers: the PR bar chart (68 to 3,127 per month, mar to oct, 10,732 total; the title's "3,000 PRs last month" matches the Oct bar of 3,127). The trust curve, loops, pyramid and weed diagram are conceptual with no data. "most people get stuck here" and "frontier" are bare assertions.
- Chart year not shown, months only. The 10,732 "total" is the sum of the eight bars (mar-oct).
- Video-maker additions: opening AI Engineer Code Summit logo animation, "Presented by Google DeepMind" card, platinum sponsors card (aws, Devin, databricks, exa, FACTORY, Sonar), conference sidebar/title bar and speaker camera. All slide content is the speaker's own hand-drawn style slides (marked @poteto).
- Chapter alignment: "Turning slop into gold" (0:00) covers slides 1-6; "The trust pyramid and codebase quality" starts at 2:02, which matches the pyramid slide appearing at 2:02.
