## Observations
- Opening (0:51-1:02): speaker re-introduces herself (poteto, Grok Bot at SpaceXSI) and does a show of hands: who has looked at someone else's code and thought "that's all slop". The title slide's "(except mine)" build lands the joke ("Well, I have too.").
- Cartoon slide (1:13): a joke contrast between "our" agent-assisted craft and "their" slop, a self-deprecating take on tribal code-quality judgments. She promises to convince the audience that slop can be turned into gold. Bare promise at this point.
- Bar chart (1:20): the only hard numbers in this segment. Claim "3,000 PRs to prod last month"; the chart shows mar 68, apr 254, may 352, jun 685, jul 1,113, aug 2,401, sep 2,732, oct 3,127, "10,732 total" (the eight bars sum to 10,732; oct 3,127 is rounded to "3,000" in speech). No definition of "PR" and no quality metric on this slide.
- Thesis (1:32-2:00): with agents anyone can be a "coding machine" (high-volume engineer; Bret Taylor rewriting Google Maps in a weekend is an unsourced anecdote). The engineer's job is now to maintain or improve code quality while massively increasing productivity. Slide subline: "the new meta is shipping fast without compromising on quality".
- Trust curve (2:00): qualitative, trust vs number of agents (1, 1 to 5, 5 to 10, 10 to 20, hundreds, thousands), concave toward a "frontier". No data points; illustrative. She prefers "all about trust" over the label "software factory", and says trust is not blind but comes from investing in the agent's environment.
- Two-loop diagram (2:09-2:34): low trust (agent writes slop, human verifies, issues found; "most people get stuck here") vs high trust (agent writes good code, agent verifies it; "you want to be here"). Claims that it took her six months to get to shipping thousands of PRs, "a lot of them in my sleep", and that others will be faster. These are bare assertions with no evidence shown.
- Channel-added material: the Korean caption line (mostly faithful; adds the gloss "(질 낮은 코드)" for slop at 1:02 and phrases the Bret Taylor anecdote as "구글 지도를 통째로 다시 짰다는 일화"); the top-left conference label; and the full-screen purple recap card at 2:40-2:51 (tag says the slide is from presentation time 1:46) with a dimmed slide, a bright-box highlight on each loop, and accumulating pill labels (여섯 달 / 저신뢰 · 사람이 검증 / 고신뢰 · 에이전트가 검증). The recap re-narrates the diagram in Korean and adds no new numbers; highlighting left then right emphasizes who does the verifying (human vs agent) as the key difference.
- Chapter note: the chapter title "한 달 PR 11,000개" corresponds to the chart's 10,732 total, rounded by the channel; the 0.5% revert figure does not appear in this segment.
