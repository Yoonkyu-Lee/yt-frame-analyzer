- Opening gag: a command literally named `scam start` "starts the camera", i.e. the on-screen "Human" webcam is framed as something a script launches - a wink at the idea that the person on video could be generated. He then shrinks and moves the webcam window to the corner.

- On the sticky note he types his reasoning live: "this video might be fake" -> "this website might be fake / AI can generate it" -> "my old website can be fake / you can buy new domain and use AI genet(erate)...". Any single artifact (video, site, even an old-looking personal site) can be forged by AI.
- He wipes the note and writes the thesis: "what AI cannot fake / that is history" (bold "history"). He goes to the Wayback Machine for wingram.org (his older site/ideology page, "Wingram on the way we find the Truth") showing 27 third-party captures from Jan 2024 to Mar 2025 and opens one from 30 Aug 2024 - a past recorded by others (archive, certificate-transparency logs) that cannot be retroactively fabricated.
- His own site hayyoth.org lays out his life choices as an "Everyone vs I" table: studied to understand the world rather than for prestige, taught himself programming, software freedom, left the company, turned to farming, explored human nature/trust/attention, "Just do whatever I want", "Quit quitting"; tagline "Understand the Game. Don't Play It Blindly. The Art of Quitting." Video list includes "Opinions on AI", "Build your own X from scratch", "Learn C", "Data Structures in C", "Lisp Language".
- Style: minimal tools (Stickies, Firefox, Terminal), notes typed live and rewritten, demonstration instead of slides. Philosophy: trust comes from verifiable history over time, not from content that can be generated.
